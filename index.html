CHAPTER 01

STATIC

✦ ✦ ✦

"Static is the sound of a body trying to remember what a soul used to feel. The chronicle of a love that has retreated entirely into the physical."

The song opens with a plea that is equal parts devotion and dread: "Baby, you haunt me. Don't make me linger. Slowly, don't leave me. Don't make it bitter." There is no anger here, only the quiet terror of someone who senses the emotional architecture collapsing and reaches for the body. "Slowly, don't leave me" is a negotiation for delay, letting the dissolution arrive in increments so the silence isn't heard all at once.

"And now she touches me under the grey light… under the daylight." Touch persists, but beneath it lies a fracture: "I will be the atom space and You won't wake up with your heart races." The narrator is becoming the empty distance between particles. The physical act remains, but the electricity that gave it meaning has drained away.

"Your skin of chest shines in a burning light. Your sweat of neck keeps on drowning me out." This is immersion so total it becomes annihilation. You press yourself against someone's body to feel close, and all you feel is how far away they are beneath the skin.

"Drag me down, down the light. Drag me down, down the line." A slow, gravitational pull along the wire where the signal used to travel, where now there is only static. "She grasps me like filaments with her body" the words are holding on brilliantly, briefly, and at the cost of itself. "Will you swing your hips like tonight? Will you sing for me and leave this party?" are questions trying to keep a dying performance alive.

"Two bodies in the same room, generating heat, producing no sound."
CHAPTER 02

MEDEA

✦ ✦ ✦

"There is a particular kind of despair that lives only inside obsession and not the absence of feeling, but its excess, burning until nothing remains but ash and silence."

There is a loneliness that no crowd can cure. The kind that finds you mid-laughter, mid-conversation, surrounded by voices that somehow never reach you. You are seen by everyone and known by no one.

And then there is the anger. Not the loud kind. The quiet, seething kind of the kind that arrives when love is offered like a debt, when care becomes a cage, when tenderness is wielded as a blade. The kind that asks: "what do I do with all this devotion that was never mine to keep?"

Medea is not a story about a villain. It is a story about what happens when a person gives everything and is met with disposal. It is the sound of someone deciding that if they cannot be loved wholly, they will not be loved at all.

This song is for every quiet rage you have swallowed. For every room full of people where you still felt like a ghost. For every love that made you smaller instead of seen.

"You don't have to chase the stars. But you are allowed to worship it."
CHAPTER 03

CORDUROY LEATHER

✦ ✦ ✦

"Corduroy Leather is the sound of an obsession recognizing its own decay. It is the moment when the object of devotion begins to lose its sacred weight."

The song opens with violence disguised as language: "Words of flesh tear my head. The glorious grace burns my way." Words are corporeal here. They tear. And grace that which should redeem, uplift, absolve, and does not descend gently. It burns. Devotion is established as an act of self-immolation: to love this completely is to be set alight by the thing you pray to.

Then comes the image that anchors the entire track: "I'm falling from the heaven you built with skin and bones." The paradise was constructed from a body, from ribs, from cartilage, from physical architecture. And now the narrator is falling from it because skin and bones were never meant to hold the weight of a sky.

"It was you that I wanted. Your skin and bones lived in me." This is not metaphor; this is genesis. To lose this person is not to lose a companion, but to lose a part of your own structural architecture. The obsession was directed inward, toward the piece of yourself that was taken and told to be someone else.

"You burned my corduroy leather... and tore my corduroy leather." Corduroy is texture; leather is skin hardened to endure. Together, they form a body mapped with scars. The obsession does not fade gracefully; it is ripped from you while you are still wearing it.

The final line addresses the beloved directly: "Keep your suffering, Goddess of Lust. It keeps pulling me in." The beloved is now a deity of hunger and the animal gravity that keeps a body orbiting something that consumes it. The mind says keep it, but the skin still reaches.

"It is the sound of peeling off a second skin and discovering that what grew underneath is not you."
CHAPTER 04

EITHER I LIVE, OR I WILL LEAVE

✦ ✦ ✦

"A love song that is also a farewell. Born not of hatred, but of the unbearable distance between being loved and feeling found."

A meditation on the most isolating form of solitude: the loneliness that persists even when one is loved. It is the paradox of being surrounded, yet utterly unseen; of being held, yet feeling as though no hand has ever reached you.

The song opens with a question that lingers like a wound that refuses to close: "Why do I always feel surrounded?" This is not the solitude of abandonment, it is the disorienting realization that presence does not guarantee connection. The beloved exists as both "the lesson" and "pure grace," yet the narrator remains bound to "the thorns that you don't know how they taste." Love is present, but its weight is carried alone.

"I'll wait until you wake up, I won't let it in when it turns up" captures the quiet exhaustion of performing stability for someone who loves you. You wait for them to see you, while refusing to let the grief breach the surface.

"Tempest, Heaven; They will not hold us both." Love, in its fullest form, is not enough to contain two people who exist in different emotional realities. One drowns while the other watches the sky. The "fractured hatred" is not directed at the beloved, it is directed at the self for bleeding in a room where someone is trying to heal you.

The second verse deepens the isolation: "The waters are coming without you, and I don't believe that they would." The narrator does not doubt the beloved's intention, only their ability to arrive in time. "The sky never turns the same blue" speaks to the irreversible alteration of perception, once you have felt alone inside a relationship, the world never looks the same again.

"There is only the repetition, the asking, the waiting for a sky that has already changed color."
