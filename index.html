CHAPTER 01

STATIC

"Static is the sound of a body trying to remember what a soul used to feel."

The song opens with a plea that is equal parts devotion and dread: "Baby, you haunt me. Don't make me linger." There is no anger here, only the quiet terror of someone who senses the emotional architecture collapsing.

"Your skin of chest shines in a burning light." This is immersion so total it becomes annihilation. You press yourself against someone's body to feel close, and all you feel is how far away they are beneath the skin.

Two bodies in the same room, generating heat, producing no sound.

CHAPTER 02

MEDEA

"There is a particular kind of despair that lives only inside obsession."

There is a loneliness that no crowd can cure. The kind that finds you mid-laughter, surrounded by voices that somehow never reach you.

Medea is not a story about a villain. It is a story about what happens when a person gives everything and is met with disposal. It is the sound of someone deciding that if they cannot be loved wholly, they will not be loved at all.

You don't have to chase the stars. But you are allowed to worship it.

CHAPTER 03

CORDUROY LEATHER

"The sound of an obsession recognizing its own decay."

"I'm falling from the heaven you built with skin and bones." The paradise was constructed from a body. And now the narrator is falling from it because skin and bones were never meant to hold the weight of a sky.

"You burned my corduroy leather..." Corduroy is texture; leather is skin hardened to endure. The obsession does not fade gracefully; it is ripped from you while you are still wearing it.

It is the sound of peeling off a second skin.

CHAPTER 04

EITHER I LIVE, OR I WILL LEAVE

"A love song that is also a farewell."

A meditation on the most isolating form of solitude: the loneliness that persists even when one is loved. It is the paradox of being surrounded, yet utterly unseen.

"Tempest, Heaven; They will not hold us both." Love, in its fullest form, is not enough to contain two people who exist in different emotional realities.

Waiting for a sky that has already changed color.
